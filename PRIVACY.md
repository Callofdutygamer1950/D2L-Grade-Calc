# Privacy Policy: Grade Calculator for D2L

_Effective November 11, 2026_

Grade Calculator for D2L ("the extension") shows your overall course grade on the D2L Brightspace Grades page. This policy explains what data the extension uses and where it goes. The extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by D2L Corporation.

## The short version

- **Your grades never leave your computer.** Scores, percentages and points are read from the Grades page and used only inside your browser.
- **Only class setups are shared, and only if sharing is on.** A "setup" is category names, item names, groups and weights. It never includes your name, email, student ID or grades.
- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

## What the extension reads

On D2L Grades pages (`*.desire2learn.com` and `*.brightspace.com`) the extension reads:

- the grades table: category names, item names, points and percentages, to calculate your grade;
- the page title and D2L's course information for that course (course code such as `CPSC-374` and the term code), to tell courses apart.

All of this is processed locally in your browser.

## What is stored on your computer

Using your browser's extension storage, the extension saves, per course: the weights and groups you set, the course's category and item names, and whether your setup came from classmates. It also stores your sharing on/off setting and a random anonymous ID (see below). This data stays in your browser and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## What is shared (sharing with classmates)

Sharing is on by default and can be turned off at any time with the **"Share my setups and use classmates' setups"** switch at the bottom of the extension's weights page.

When sharing is on:

- **When you set or change weights or groups for a course**, the extension sends this to our database:
  - the D2L site address (for example `school.desire2learn.com`) and the course's D2L ID number,
  - the course code and term code (for example `CPSC-374` and `202630`), if available,
  - your setup: category names and their weights, and which uncategorized items you put in which group (item and group names),
  - a random install ID generated by the extension. It's not linked to your name, email, D2L account or device; it only lets your later changes replace your earlier setup instead of counting twice.
- **When you open a course you haven't set up**, the extension asks the database for the setup most students shared for that course (sending only the site address, course ID, course code and term code) and uses it for you.

The database is hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com). It only accepts data in the format above and rejects anything else. Other users of the extension can receive a course's most popular setup (category names, item names, groups and weights) and the number of students who shared it. They never see install IDs or who shared what.

When sharing is off, nothing is uploaded or downloaded.

## How long shared data is kept, and how to remove it

Your shared setup for a course is kept until you change it or remove it. To remove it, with sharing still on, clear your weights and reset your groups for that course on the weights page; the extension then deletes your entry. You can also email us (below) to ask for removal of shared data.

## How the data is used

Shared setups are used only to give other students of the same course a starting set of weights and groups. They are not sold, not used for advertising, not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending, and not used for any purpose other than this extension's single purpose of calculating course grades.

## Children

The extension is meant for students using D2L Brightspace and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
